With my PhD students, we are working on exciting problems on sparse and memory/energy efficient neural networks for learning in large output spaces and Large Language Models (LLMs). I am also looking for motivated PhD students to join my group at the University of Bath, UK. Please reach out at rb2608--AT--bath.ac.uk, if you are interested in joining!

Efficient machine learning at extreme scale

I am Rohit Babbar, a Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) at the University of Bath. My group develops resource-efficient learning methods for large neural networks, large output spaces and large language models.

Our research spans sparse training, low-precision computation, hardware-aware algorithms, extreme multi-label classification and efficient LLM inference.

Research

Research themes

My work focuses on machine-learning methods that remain accurate, scalable and deployable under demanding computational constraints.

01

Efficient large language models

Sparse, low-precision and speculative methods for reducing the memory and inference cost of large language models.

Speculative decoding · Sparse fine-tuning · Low precision

02

Hardware-aware sparse learning

Sparsity structures and training algorithms designed to translate theoretical operation reductions into practical accelerator speedups.

Dynamic sparsity · GPU efficiency · Structured sparsity

03

Learning in extreme output spaces

Scalable classification, retrieval and recommendation methods for problems involving hundreds of thousands or millions of outputs.

Extreme classification · Retrieval · Long-tail learning
